Title: New Hen Cove Boat Ramp Slated For Completion
Author: HELEN SIEVERT
Date: 2026-06-12T00:00:00-04:00
URL: https://www.capenews.net/bourne/news/new-hen-cove-boat-ramp-slated-for-completion/article_4295125d-3030-49d4-838e-4c80e6c426c2.html

Bourne Department of Natural Resources Director Hannah Lawrence announced the end date for the Hen Cove ramp project in Pocasset at the Select Board’s meeting on Tuesday , June 9 .

“We do have an end date coming up for the Hen Cove boat ramp,” Lawrence said. “That’ll be all done on Friday and it’ll be available for use.”

The project to construct a new boat ramp was undertaken as “the previous ramp was unusable as there was not sufficient water depth to accommodate launching a boat much larger than a dinghy,” Lawrence wrote in an email to the Enterprise on Wednesday , June 10 . It “is slated for completion at the end of the week,” she added.

The project was funded by the annual shore and harbor dredging, ramp and pier repair article, which itself is funded by the Waterways Improvement Fund.

Established by municipalities, the Waterways Improvement Fund houses the revenue from boat excise taxes and fees and penalties associated with mooring permits and sums from the state and federal government.

Section 5G of the state general law outlines that the monies in the fund can be used for “maintenance, dredging, cleaning and improvement of harbors, inland waters and great ponds, the public access thereto, the breakwaters, retaining walls, piers, wharves and moorings thereof, and law enforcement and fire prevention.”

“This project will allow for better access for the community and more evenly distribute the ramp usage and should help in alleviating some of the volume at the Barlow’s Landing ramp,” Lawrence added on Wednesday.
