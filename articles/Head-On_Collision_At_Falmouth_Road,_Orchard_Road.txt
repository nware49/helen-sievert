Title: Head-On Collision At Falmouth Road, Orchard Road
Author: HELEN SIEVERT
Date: 2026-05-10T11:11:00-04:00
URL: https://www.capenews.net/police_fire/head-on-collision-at-falmouth-road-orchard-road/article_3873c6c3-a5d0-470b-a6ea-379cb4ddb18c.html

The Mashpee Fire and Police departments were dispatched after a report of a two-vehicle, head-on crash at the intersection of Falmouth and Orchard roads on Sunday , May 10 , at around 9:17 AM.

Upon arriving at the scene first responders found one vehicle, which had been traveling eastbound on Falmouth Road, in the woods against a tree and the other, which had allegedly been turning onto Falmouth Road, in the intersection.

Both vehicles were occupied solely by the drivers, with only the driver of the vehicle in the woods being transported to Cape Cod Hospital for evaluation.
