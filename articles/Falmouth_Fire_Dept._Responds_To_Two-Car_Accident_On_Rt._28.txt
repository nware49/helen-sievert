Title: Falmouth Fire Dept. Responds To Two-Car Accident On Rt. 28
Author: HELEN SIEVERT
Date: 2026-06-27T12:44:00-04:00
URL: https://www.capenews.net/police_fire/falmouth-fire-dept-responds-to-two-car-accident-on-rt-28/article_4e99fbd9-7671-4b16-aadc-0b780212cdd2.html

The Falmouth Fire and Rescue Department responded to a report of a motor-vehicle collision at East Falmouth Highway and Muriel Lane, at 11:16 AM on Saturday, June 27.

After arriving at the scene, responders found two motor vehicles, a pick-up truck and a sedan, and observed moderate damage to the sedan.

One adult, with minor injuries, was transported to Falmouth Hospital.
