Title: Bourne To Open Cooling Center Due To Hot Temperatures
Author: HELEN SIEVERT
Date: 2026-06-29T17:05:00-04:00
URL: https://www.capenews.net/bourne/briefs/bourne-to-open-cooling-center-due-to-hot-temperatures/article_34b623fc-6e85-4767-b7c9-194478888962.html

On Monday, June 29, the Town of Bourne announced that it will open a cooling center at the Bourne Police Department, located at 35 Armory Road in Buzzards Bay, beginning on Tuesday, June 30, until Friday, July 3.

Due to the “high heat indexes expected,” the town stated that the center would be open from 8 AM to 8 PM from Tuesday through Thursday and 8 AM to 3 PM on Friday to provide “air conditioning, water, and charging stations.”

The notice provided tips to “stay cool and safe,” which included the following: “Drink plenty of water throughout the day even if you are not thirsty; Stay in air-conditioned areas as much as possible; Avoid strenuous outdoor activities during the hottest part of the day; If outside, use sunscreen (SPF 30 or higher), wear a wide-brimmed hat, and take frequent breaks in the shade or air conditioning; Wear lightweight, light-colored, loose-fitting clothing with hats if outdoors; Never leave children, older adults, or pets in parked vehicles, even for a few minutes. Interior temperatures can become deadly, quickly; and Protect pets by providing fresh water, shade, and avoid having them walk on hot pavement.”

For updates, the town encourages people to visit the Police Department’s Facebook page.
