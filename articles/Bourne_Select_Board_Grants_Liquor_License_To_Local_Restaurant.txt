Title: Bourne Select Board Grants Liquor License To Local Restaurant
Author: HELEN SIEVERT
Date: 2026-04-03T00:00:00-04:00
URL: https://www.capenews.net/bourne/news/bourne-select-board-grants-liquor-license-to-local-restaurant/article_398e2ab8-a87d-49fb-91f3-2edb40e098f8.html

Taste & Tide Restaurant, Inc. received a new annual wine and malt beverages on premises liquor license pending passing the final building and fire inspections, following a public hearing during the Bourne Select Board’s meeting on Tuesday , March 31 .

Located at 18 Beach Street in Monument Beach, the restaurant is approximately 1,250 square feet, including a fenced-in 100-square-foot outdoor area and features seating for 42 people indoors and four tables outside. The restaurant already holds a common victualler license.

Seeking a liquor license to expand offerings at the restaurant in addition to serving homemade food, restaurant manager Raquel Pereira operates the establishment alongside her friends. She added that they have around 20 to 25 years of experience and were based in Marshfield and Hanover.

Taste & Tide “started a little bit before last summer,” Pereira said, adding that over the course of the past year, they have become more accustomed to the area and “really like that spot.”

“It is a great little spot…nice little community of people,” Select Board vice chairwoman Anne-Marie Siroonian said. “We appreciate you coming in and growing a business in our town.”

Select Board member Mary Jane Mastrangelo stated that a liquor license will provide “a great addition for your menu.” Select Board member Peter J. Meier agreed, noting that providing more offerings will help the business.

Since no public comments were made, the public hearing was closed. The board then unanimously voted to approve the application, pending the final inspections.
