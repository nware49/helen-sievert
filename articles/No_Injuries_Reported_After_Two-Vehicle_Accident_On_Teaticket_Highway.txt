Title: No Injuries Reported After Two-Vehicle Accident On Teaticket Highway
Author: HELEN SIEVERT
Date: 2026-06-28T17:03:00-04:00
URL: https://www.capenews.net/police_fire/no-injuries-reported-after-two-vehicle-accident-on-teaticket-highway/article_1c63e134-2b81-4871-aa11-21ad7b45d82e.html

On Sunday, June 28, at 3:58 PM, the Falmouth Fire Rescue Department responded to a report of a motor vehicle collision at 485 Teaticket Highway, near the Great Bay Motel.

Upon arriving at the scene, responders found that a pick-up truck and a car had collided and observed moderate damage to both vehicles. No injuries were reported.

Minor hazards in the roadway were also addressed by personnel.
