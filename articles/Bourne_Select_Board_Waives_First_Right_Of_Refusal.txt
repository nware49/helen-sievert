Title: Bourne Select Board Waives First Right Of Refusal
Author: HELEN SIEVERT
Date: 2026-07-03T00:00:00-04:00
URL: https://www.capenews.net/bourne/news/bourne-select-board-waives-first-right-of-refusal/article_19b2a42b-9e67-412b-95f8-c12cce880ccf.html

The Select Board unanimously voted to not exercise its right of first refusal to purchase a .97-acre portion of land after the property’s owners sent the town a notice of intent to sell, pursuant to Chapter 61A, Section 14 of the state’s general laws.

The land, which is located within the parcel at 665 Scenic Highway and 0 Bournedale Road, falls under that state statute through its agricultural use.

In the notice of intent to sell, the sellers, trustees of the Panhandle Trust, state that the buyers, NextGrid Energy LLC, which is a renewable power company, propose to convert the land’s use from agricultural and horticultural to commercial, industrial or residential. The asking price for the land is $600,000.

At the board’s last meeting on the matter on June 9, the board had decided to ask the Bourne Conservation Commission, Assessor’s Office, Planning Board and Open Space Committee to notify the Select Board whether or not they have an interest in the town purchasing the land.

Responses to the board stated that there was no interest, resulting in the board’s vote to waive its opportunity to purchase the land.
