Title: Vehicle Crashes Into Redemption Center At Kenyon’s Market
Author: HELEN SIEVERT
Date: 2026-06-27T18:52:00-04:00
URL: https://www.capenews.net/police_fire/vehicle-crashes-into-redemption-center-at-kenyon-s-market/article_3f6fe250-ba2c-4e18-9ce9-9a70ee0385ec.html

On Saturday, June 27, at 4:58 PM, the Falmouth Fire and Rescue Department responded to a report of a motor vehicle accident at Kenyon’s Market located at 769 East Falmouth Highway.

Responders found that a single vehicle had struck the redemption center, and also observed minor damage to both the structure and vehicle. The redemption center is closed.

One adult, the driver of the vehicle, was transported to a local hospital for evaluation.
