Three-Car Accident On MacArthur Boulevard Southbound

By Helen Sievert

June 28, 2026

At 11 AM, the Bourne Fire and Rescue Department responded to a report of a motor vehicle accident on MacArthur Boulevard heading southbound, on Sunday, June 28.

The crash involved three motor vehicles, and occurred along MacArthur Boulevard in the vicinity of the Battles GMC dealership.

One individual was transported to a local hospital with non-life threatening injuries.
