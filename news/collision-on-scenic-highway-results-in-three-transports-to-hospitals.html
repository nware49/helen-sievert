Collision On Scenic Highway Results In Three Transports To Hospitals

By Helen Sievert

July 26, 2026

Bourne Police and Fire departments were dispatched at approximately 6:48 AM on Sunday, July 26, to a report of a motor vehicle accident on Scenic Highway near the Herring Run Rest Area.

Upon arriving at the scene, responders found two vehicles—a white 2026 GMC Terrain and a tan 2016 Jeep Wrangler—in the intersection at Scenic Highway and Herring Pond Road.

An initial investigation found that the GMC, operated by a 41-year-old man from Providence, Rhode Island, was “traveling eastbound on Scenic Hwy and failed to stop for the red light” when it “T-boned” the Jeep Wrangler as it was “traveling westbound and turning left into the Herring Run Rest Area,” the Bourne Police Department stated in a press release.

The impact of the crash resulted in the Jeep, which was operated by a 61-year-old man, from Kingston, New York, “to become airborne, roll over once, coming to rest on its wheels,” the Bourne Police said.

Sustaining possibly life-threatening injuries, the operator of the GMC was Medflighted to an area trauma hospital after responders had applied “a tourniquet to his right upper arm to control arterial bleeding,” police reported.

It was also shared in the press release that the passenger of the GMC, a 44-year old male from Brockton, was transported to another area trauma hospital by ambulance for “serious, but non-life threatening injuries.”

Appearing to have “minor non-life threatening injuries,” the operator of the Jeep was brought to a different hospital in the area, the police said.

The incident remains under investigation.
