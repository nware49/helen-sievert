Cataumet Fish Liquor License Review Continues

By Helen Sievert

April 10, 2026

Town Counsel Bryan F. Bertram on Tuesday , April 7, updated the Select Board on the language of the special legislation liquor license for Cataumet Fish.

Following the last discussion on March 17 that the Select Board and Bertram had on the matter, Bertram had a conversation with the legislative delegation to explain the town’s position to remain consistent with the affirmative motion made at Town Meeting last fall.

The board sought to ensure that Bill H5084 properly reflected the vote taken at the October 2025 Special Town Meeting in favor of having the Select Board petition the Legislature to issue a “nontransferable, annual, all-alcohol package store license specific to Sea State Foods, LLC, Patrick Ross, Manager, d/b/a Cataumet Fish.” Through its legislative delegation, the town submitted a request for that action. The Legislature had sent the bill back with proposed revisions for the Select Board to review.

The bill aims to authorize the Town of Bourne to grant a nontransferable license to sell alcohol for off-premises consumption, the Massachusetts Legislature website states.

Since the Legislature was “just trying to standardize their language in the State House, when they do these kinds of special concerns,” Bertram said, “at present, it’s probably not going to be a problem if we want to push back” on the revised draft from the State House and “change it.”

Noting that the Select Board had some concern regarding the transferability language in the revised draft by the Legislature, which states that the license is “not transferable as to the location,” Bertram stated that the board has two options to consider.

“I think we should specify,” he said. The first option would be to “drop off the location and just say ‘not transferable.’” The second option would have the draft state that the license be nontransferable to “any other person, organization, corporation or location.”

Bertram added that this decision would be under the purview of the Select Board to decide if including that language aligns with Town Meeting’s vote.

Another point of discussion concerned the language for granting a license to a new applicant at the same location. Bertram stated that the counsel for the owners of Cataumet Fish, Patrick and Jennifer Ross, had contacted him to share that “they would be in favor of keeping that, on the logic that it wouldn’t be a transfer, but it would give the town discretion if in the future this business were to wind down and we still wanted to have a liquor license in that location.”

Select Board vice chairwoman Anne-Marie Siroonian stated that a nontransferable license is “an absolute,” citing “that was the intent and how we presented it to Town Meeting and how people understood that vote.”

She noted that it was explained at Town Meeting that “when this individual, entity or corporation no longer exists, that the license would no longer exist.” Select Board member Peter J. Meier agreed with Siroonian.

Meier added that if the Rosses would “want to change it and go back to Town Meeting with a different special act, that’s one thing, but the intent—I want to stay to what was approved at last year’s Town Meeting.”

“I definitely agree with no transfer to a different location,” Select Board member Mary Jane Mastrangelo stated.

“I’m just cautious because it is a special license, and to transfer it or get a new one, you would have to go through special legislation,” Mastrangelo stated, noting that “the wisdom of the Legislature, in terms of the limits of the transfer,” may arise in “some situations.” She continued stating, “I am not sure about a transfer of manager with the same entity at the same location.” Mastrangelo added that she would like to provide the Rosses an opportunity to speak before the board.

Jeanne L. Azarovitz, Select Board clerk, also agreed with Bertram’s first option to have the transferability language state that the license is “not transferable,” as well as Meier’s point of staying aligned with the intent of Town Meeting.

Regarding the granting of a license to a new applicant at the same location, Azarovitz said: “It would be wise if the applicant were here for that discussion as well.”

Select Board chairwoman Melissa A. Ferretti echoed the statements made by Meier, Siroonian and Azarovitz, citing the intent of Town Meeting’s vote.

“Do we get a meeting to ask Patrick [Ross] to come in?” Ferretti asked. “If they’re pretty firm on having it transferable, they may need to re-petition or do whatever they need to.”

Bertram said he would contact the Rosses through their counsel to schedule a time for them to discuss the matter with the board.
