Bourne Select Board Approves Licenses for New Golf Simulation Business

By Helen Sievert

May 15, 2026

Another step was taken for the proposed golf simulator business on MacArthur Boulevard, as the Bourne Select Board approved an annual general on-premises all alcoholic beverages license and a new annual entertainment license.

James E. Stephen McCarthy, manager of The Clubhouse LLC, spoke before the board at its meeting on Tuesday , May 12, about requesting the two licenses for the new business.

An entertainment license is required for the proposed indoor live music, two billiard tables, one virtual reality golf station and five golf simulator bays.

“We think golfers may like to have a beverage,” McCarthy said, explaining the request for the general on-premises all alcoholic beverages license.

Located at 688 MacArthur Boulevard, at the intersection of MacArthur Boulevard and Barlows Landing Road, the 4,000-square-foot stand-alone, single-story building will feature a lounge area, five golf simulator bays, a storage area and two restrooms. It had previously housed a Dunkin’ franchise.

The new golf simulation business would be open approximately “16 hours a day, seven days a week,” McCarthy said, adding that he hopes the new business would create “eight to 10 new jobs.”

The golf simulators, made by Trackman, will offer more than 300 golf courses for people to choose from, as well as kids’ games.

“Each of the five bays will have their own bar and seating, so that other players can have a place to sit down while the players shoot,” McCarthy said.

It was noted by McCarthy that the business would also provide “a new entertainment venue for an activity that doesn’t exist right now” in Bourne that would be available day and night, year-round, for golfers, gatherings and people “looking for something different to do, more than just sitting in a bar.”

“It’ll be treated like a golf club,” McCarthy stated, explaining that there would be a full-time professional to organize leagues, tournaments and lessons. He also hoped to expand these offerings to youth.

In addition to providing a self-service coffee station with prepackaged baked goods and a refrigerator with cold sandwiches, wraps, salads, and beverages, he also explained a proposed collaboration with JD Prime Pizza, which is also on MacArthur Boulevard

“We’ll be able to place orders on our tablet, which will print out there, he will prepare the food, and we will have dedicated drivers bring it over,” McCarthy said.

The business has gotten Board of Health and ServSafe Alcohol certifications, he stated.

There were no public comments made.

The application was well received by the Select Board, with vice chairwoman Anne-Marie Siroonian stating it is “a great concept,” sharing how she had enjoyed a personal experience at Pebble Beach, which is in California, through a golf simulator.

Siroonian also inquired about the various other games that Trackman offers: “You could choose soccer if you wanted?” McCarthy replied, “Yeah, they have some kids’ games, target practice and so on and so forth.”

“It’s healthy entertainment in a safe environment,” Siroonian stated.

Clerk Jeanne L. Azarovitz appreciated McCarthy’s description of the proposed layout and plan for the business, adding that “this is a great idea.”

She also asked if the business would be “taking over that entire building,” which McCarthy confirmed. He added that “there’s actually a larger bay, which used to be the real estate office–so that will be available for birthday parties, et cetera.”

“Okay, so separate,” Azarovitz commented.

“It sounds like a great idea. It’s a great location,” member Mary Jane Mastrangelo said.

Chairwoman Melissa A. Ferretti also thought it was a “great idea,” noting how “people want to go somewhere they can do something unique.”

The Select Board closed the public hearing and unanimously approved a motion to approve the application for both licenses, and to “authorize and direct town staff and town counsel to prepare correspondence memorializing the same for transmission to the applicant and to the Alcohol Beverage Control Commission.”
