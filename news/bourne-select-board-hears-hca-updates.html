Bourne Select Board Hears HCA Updates

By Helen Sievert

July 3, 2026

The Bourne Select Board received updates at its meeting on June 30 from the three cannabis dispensaries in town that were awarded a social equity host community agreement (HCA) with the town: Capeway Cannabis, Bourne Cannabis and Chief Cannabis.

As outlined in the town’s social equity policy, the purpose of the HCA was to encourage “diversity in the local cannabis industry.”

Kevin Hough of Capeway Cannabis announced that the store is open as of June 25. He shared that after organizing a career fair, the business was able to hire employees, 80% of whom are Bourne residents and 65% women.

“We’re up and running and doing great,” he added.

Michael Kinahan of Bourne Cannabis shared that the business has been “on schedule” and the goal is to open the business in April 2027. In the next couple of weeks, he shared that the business will apply for a provisional license from the Cannabis Control Commission (CCC).

Having been before the Board of Health in April and the Planning Board in May, Allen shared that he received the architectural plans last week. Once the business has signed with a general contractor, the hope is to obtain a building permit in the coming weeks in order to begin construction, with the goal being completion in either December or January.

Michael Allen of Chief Cannabis shared that he is anticipating opening in August. The business has currently received a provisional license from CCC and completed its first inspection with the CCC, Allen said, with the hope of receiving the final license and inspection in July.

A general manager is “just about” on board, Allen said, adding that they are organizing a career fair to be held in the next couple of weeks.
