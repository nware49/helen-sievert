Bourne Select Board Approves License For Bakery

By Helen Sievert

May 15, 2026

Buzzards Bay’s Main Street is one step closer to having Uncle Jon’s Cookies.

Jonathan Pope, the owner of Uncle Jon’s Cookies, had submitted an application for a new annual common victualler license for the business, which was approved by the Bourne Select Board at its meeting on Tuesday , May 12 .

Located at 85 Main Street, Unit 93, Uncle Jon’s will be a coffee shop and bakery and therefore requires the Select Board’s approval for a common victualler license.

“Any establishment that has on its premises the ability to assemble, prepare, or cook food and has seating for the purpose of consuming that food on the premises,” the Town of Bourne states on its website.

Select Board member Mary Jane Mastrangelo inquired when the business would be opening.

Having submitted the required documents to the Town of Bourne, the remaining steps involve final inspections and approvals, Pope said.

“Well, I think I’ve got to make sure I go through all motions. I almost completed everything on the inside. We’ve been inspected, we’ve passed the plumbing, electrical has been in; I don’t know if that has passed yet,” he added. “We’re ready for the Fire Department and Board of Health, so we just have to wait for those steps to be completed.”

The application summary form states comments by various town departments in relation to the matter. The Building Department states that it has approved the permit, which is “contingent on the passing of the final inspection.” The clerk’s office has issued the business certificate. The Health Department wrote that, when ready, the business should contact the Health Department for a pre-operational inspection. The Sewer Department noted that a change of use was “approved by the Sewer Commissioners” on August 19, 2025.

The collector’s office, police, fire and planning departments all had no comments on the application summary form.

“We just feel very welcomed into the community,” Pope said of his experience so far. “And Town Hall’s been wonderful.”

Pope owns Uncle Jon’s Coffees, which has locations in Mattapoisett and Marion.

The board unanimously approved granting the license to the business.
