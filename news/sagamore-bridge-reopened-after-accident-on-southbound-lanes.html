Sagamore Bridge Reopened After Accident On Southbound Lanes

By Helen Sievert

April 14, 2026

The two southbound lanes of the Sagamore Bridge that were closed are now reopened. The lanes had been closed due to a two-car accident on the Cape-side of the bridge that occurred Tuesday morning, April 14.

At approximately 10:15 AM, state troopers and Bourne Fire/EMS responded to Route 6, near exit 1C, after receiving a report of a two-car accident wherein one car rolled over.

One adult was transported to a hospital for treatment, and three adults were treated for non-life threatening injuries, the state police said in a statement to the Enterprise.
