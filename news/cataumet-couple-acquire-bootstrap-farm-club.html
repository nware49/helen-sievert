Cataumet Couple Acquire Bootstrap Farm Club

By Helen Sievert

April 23, 2026

A new chapter under new ownership is underway for the Bootstrap Farm Club as it reopened on April 16 for another season of providing local and organic food to the Upper Cape.

Christopher and Kristin Bergeron, high school sweethearts and lifelong Cataumet residents, decided to throw their hats in the ring after Susan Sigel, Bootstrap’s founder, said she would be retiring and seeking to hand over ownership of the store.

Describing their interest as “instantaneous,” Kristin said how “it seemed right from the very second that we saw it.” Christopher added that every night on his way home from work, he would park in Bootstrap’s parking lot to imagine the possibility and gauge if it “feels right.”

“It definitely does,” he said.

Sigel said the Bergerons encapsulate who she envisioned would be the new owners of the store due to their shared passion for maintaining the store’s ethos as a local and organic food store, connection to the community, retail experience in light of the business’s fast pace and many moving pieces, and being a husband-wife team.

Sigel established Bootstrap in May 2019 after moving from Martha’s Vineyard to Cataumet. “My original vision was something teeny tiny, so we are much bigger than I ever originally thought we might be,” she said, noting the store developed into an important food source for the community during the COVID-19 pandemic. The store offers a variety of foods, such as seasonal crops, meats, pies, eggs, fermented and pickled vegetables, dairy products, honey, pasta and tofu, to name a few.

“There is stuff here for everyone,” Christopher said.

Over the years, the store has developed relationships with 10 to 12 farms and 140 vendors and makers. “We strive to stay within 100 square miles,” Sigel said, explaining her tiered approach to first sourcing local organic food, then local sustainable food, followed by local foods and organic foods from a bit farther away.

“It really comes back to the importance of supporting your local community, buying local, shopping local, eating local,” Sigel added. “That keeps all that money in the community.”

The local food system has also evolved and continues to grow, she said, as “people are becoming more and more aware of the importance of eating local and eating clean and supporting sustainable local food” and visit the store because “they feel like it is a place that they can always come and ask questions about food” in regard to being conscious about what food they consume, whether it is due to wanting to eat cleaner, their health, dietary restrictions or allergies.

Offering organic foods and making it accessible to the community is a mission the Bergerons seek to continue.

“We have always been passionate about the food that we eat, trying to get as clean as we can,” Kristin said. “We have always wanted to give our kids as natural an experience as we could afford to, so it is exciting to be able to give that opportunity to other people around here in our community.”

This will be the couple’s first project together. Kristin, who works as a library teacher at North Falmouth Elementary School, will assist behind the scenes during the school year and support in-store retail at the Bootstrap during the summer months. With more than 35 years of retail sales experience, most recently managing CB Stark Jewelers in Edgartown, Christopher plans to draw on his background to run and grow the store.

Noting his pivot from selling jewelry to organic foods, Christopher said he is committed to learning more from Sigel about various aspects of the business, ranging from crops and the farms to the customers and inventory, but noted that the importance of developing “interpersonal relationships with clients, consistency, kindness, doing what you say you are going to do,” extends beyond different industries.

“Learn. That is my plan. Learn, learn, learn,” he said.

The Bergerons said they are looking forward to supporting the community, providing clean local foods and developing relationships with the farmers.

In addition to their shared passion for providing local organic foods, Sigel and the Bergerons became aware that they are connected by various “invisible strings” that have revealed themselves over the course of the past couple of months, such as the fact that CB Stark Jewelers had been a client of the advertising agency that Sigel previously operated on Martha’s Vineyard, as well as the fact that when Sigel moved to Cataumet, she moved into the same house where Kristin’s grandparents, Tom and Lucile Haslam, had lived for approximately 73 years.

Christopher also recounted stories from his mother-in-law about how she and her siblings would look forward to getting food at the Village Barn, a local restaurant located where the Bootstrap now stands.

Sigel said she will continue to assist the Bergerons during this transitional period until the end of June. She will join her son in Europe, as she plans to retire in Lille, France, where she is looking forward to enjoying the lifestyle and food culture, especially the local food markets.

“We are so blessed that she has been staying with us and walking us through this to make sure that the transition is seamless, not only for us, but the customer base too,” Kristin said. “They know that they are going to still receive the same level of care and quality when they come here.”

“It just clicks,” Christopher said of the Bootstrap, adding, “I had an old watchmaker many years ago tell me, ‘If it ain’t broke, don’t fix it.’”

The Bootstrap Farm Club is at 1 Scraggy Neck Extension in Cataumet. From April to June and September to January, the store will be open Wednesday through Sunday from 10 AM to 6 PM. During July and August, it will be open Tuesday through Sunday from 10 AM to 6 PM.

The store’s membership, which also serves as the buying base, has first access to the store’s inventory.

Bootstrap will again be operating its mobile farm stand this year at the YMCA in Wareham, HotYoga 1620 in Plymouth and Highfield Hall in Falmouth.

For more information about Bootstrap Food Club, visit bootstrapfarm.com .
