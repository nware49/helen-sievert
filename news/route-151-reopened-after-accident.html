Route 151 Reopened After Accident

By Helen Sievert

March 28, 2026

A two car-accident occurred on Friday afternoon, March 27, around 754 Nathan Ellis Highway (Route 151). Around 2:47 PM, Falmouth Police and Fire/Emergency Departments responded to a call reporting the crash.

Two transports were requested: one for a Medflight to an off-Cape trauma center and another for a ground transport to Cape Cod Trauma Center.

Part of Nathan Ellis Highway, between Ranch Road and Sam Turner, was closed as first responders assessed the scene. The road reopened later in the afternoon.
