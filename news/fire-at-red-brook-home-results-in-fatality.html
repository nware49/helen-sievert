Fire At Red Brook Home Results In Fatality

By Helen Sievert

July 25, 2026

An early morning fire in a single-family home at 15 Red Brook Road in East Falmouth resulted in a fatality on Friday, July 24.

The Falmouth Fire Department responded to the home at approximately 6:30 AM after a neighbor called 9-1-1 to notify emergency services. Upon arrival, responders encountered “heavy fire showing from the rear of the building and heavy smoke from the front door,” the Falmouth Fire-Rescue stated in a press release on Facebook.

Firefighters, with the help of the Falmouth Police Department, were able to remove a man in his 70s from the home and engage in lifesaving efforts. The man was pronounced dead at the scene. The Office of the Chief Medical Examiner will further examine the “cause and manner of his death,” the Falmouth Fire-Rescue stated.

The “bulk of the fire” was suppressed quickly, the Falmouth Fire-Rescue said; however, the home will “be a total loss.”

The home is a ranch with three bedrooms and two baths, according to real estate records. The total square footage of the home is 1,472.

The Joint Base Cape Cod, Mashpee and Sandwich fire departments also assisted, providing station coverage and direct support.

The fire is under investigation by the Falmouth Fire Department, Falmouth Police Department, State Police fire investigators assigned to the State Fire Marshal’s office and State Police assigned to the Cape & Islands District Attorney’s office.
