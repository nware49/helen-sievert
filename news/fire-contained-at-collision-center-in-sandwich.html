Fire Contained At Collision Center In Sandwich

By Helen Sievert

May 10, 2026

The Sandwich Fire Department responded to a report of a building fire at Rotary Collision Center at 336 Route 130 on May 7 at 1:37 PM.

Upon arriving on the scene, responders encountered fire from the rear of the building and heavy smoke and located a working fire. The fire was contained in one bay at the collision center, with smoke damage observed in the rest of the building, a Sandwich Fire Department news release stated.

The business was operational at the time of the fire; no injuries were reported.

The majority of the fire was extinguished In 15 minutes. An additional hour was spent extinguishing hot spots, as well as debris outside the building, the release stated.

Twenty-four firefighters responded to the fire and remained on scene for 90 minutes, with the Joint Base Cape Cod and Mashpee fire departments assisting. The Cotuit, Bourne and Barnstable fire departments provided station coverage during the incident.

“The cause of the fire is currently under investigation by the Sandwich Fire Department and Massachusetts State Police and is not considered suspicious at this time,” the Sandwich Fire Department stated in the release.
