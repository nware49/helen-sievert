Car Versus Motorbike Accident On Falmouth Road

By Helen Sievert

June 27, 2026

Around 9:22 AM on Saturday, June 27, the Mashpee Fire Department was dispatched after a report of a motor vehicle-motorbike accident at the intersection of Falmouth Road and Bowdoin Road.

As the motor vehicle was turning left from Bowdoin Road onto Falmouth Road, it had made contact with the motor bike. The accident was minor.

Upon arriving at the scene, responders found the driver of the motor bike to be standing and not expressing complaints. No transportations to the hospital occurred.
