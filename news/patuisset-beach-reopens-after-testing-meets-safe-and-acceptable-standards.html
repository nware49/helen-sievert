Patuisset Beach Reopens After Testing Meets 'Safe And Acceptable Standards'

By Helen Sievert

July 1, 2026

Patuisset Beach was reopened on Wednesday , July 1 , after testing revealed that the water quality had improved.

“Results received this afternoon confirm that bacteria levels have returned to safe and acceptable standards. Based on these findings, the temporary swimming advisory has been lifted,” Bourne Health Director Kaitlyn Shea wrote in the notice posted on the town’s website on Wednesday. “Patuisset Beach is now open and safe for recreational water use.”

Overlooking Hen Cove in Pocasset, the beach had temporarily been closed since June 19 after routine weekly testing had revealed an elevated presence of fecal coliform bacteria.

The water is tested daily after the excessive levels of bacteria are first detected, Bourne Health Department Assistant Director Stacey Burgess said on June 24.

The daily tests had primarily looked at two values: the number of bacteria and the geometric mean. Also referred to as geo-mean, it “is calculated using the five most recent samples and is intended to reflect typical conditions,” the Massachusetts Department of Public Health website states.

Information and updates about beach conditions in Bourne can be found on the town’s website at bourne-ma.gov/ and on the state’s Department of Health website at mass.gov/info-details/interactive-beach-water-quality-dashboard .
