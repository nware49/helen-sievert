No Injuries Reported In Seacoast Shores Boulevard House Fire

By Helen Sievert

July 26, 2026

A fire at a home on Seacoast Shores Boulevard in East Falmouth led to both the Falmouth and Joint Base Cape Cod fire departments to respond on Saturday, July 25.

Responders were dispatched for a reported structure fire upon receiving a 9-1-1 call at 12:19 PM. After arriving at 37 Seacoast Shores Boulevard, they observed smoke and the right side of a single-level, single-family home on fire.

The fire appeared to have begun “from an exterior source, spread to the side, and entered the attic,” the Falmouth Fire & Rescue stated in a press release.

Falmouth Fire Chief Jeffrey S. Lewis wrote in an email to the Enterprise on Sunday, July 26, that “the origin of the fire was an exterior heat source that appears to have caused refuse in a can to ignite. Once the fire grew it extended to the wall, into the void space in the wall, and to the attic where it rapidly spread.”

After extinguishing the fire on the exterior and in the attic space, which stopped the spread to the rest of the house, the firefighters “removed insulation from the attic, and completed overhaul to ensure there was no hidden fire,” the Falmouth Fire & Rescue reported. Lewis noted that this was necessary as “the home had insulation of a combustible nature with small spot fires” and removing it would “reduce the potential for later reignition from concealed fire or embers.”

In addition to the JBCC Fire Department providing assistance with the “overhaul operation,” Lewis added that “surrounding jurisdictions provided station coverage.” The Falmouth Police Department also assisted on scene.

No injuries, neither civilian or firefighter personnel, were reported. No pets were home at the time of the fire.
