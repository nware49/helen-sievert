Fire Damages Highview Condominium Unit, Owner Escapes Safely

By Helen Sievert

March 29, 2026

On Sunday morning, March 29, the Sandwich Fire Department responded to Highview Condominiums at 9:25 AM, after the owner of Unit 58 reported smoke and an activated fire alarm in the unit.

Initially encountering an electrical odor and smoke, responders located a small fire behind the sink in the floorboard, which was extinguished.

“There was moderate smoke damage in the effected unit,” the Sandwich Fire Department stated in a press release. “But no additional smoke or water damage to the rest of the building.”

Twenty-four firefighters responded to the fire and remained on scene for 90 minutes, with Bourne Fire Department assisting. Joint Base Fire, Plymouth Fire and Falmouth Fire departments provided station coverage during the incident.

No injuries were reported, as the unit owner and her two cats were removed safely. However, since the unit could not be occupied, the American Red Cross responded on the scene to assist with providing housing to the unit owner.

The cause of the fire, which is believed to be “electrical in nature,” is currently under investigation by the Sandwich Fire Department, stated the press release.
