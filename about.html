Helen Sievert's work sits where research, reporting, and public service meet. It spans an honors thesis on the political engagement of second-generation immigrants, a newsroom, a law office, and an internship with Massachusetts legislature.

She graduated from the Commonwealth Honors College at the University of Massachusetts Amherst with a B.A. in Political Science, a minor in History, and an International Relations Certificate. Her academic writing spans immigration and identity, media and democracy, foreign policy, international security, and political theory.

Today she writes and copies for The Enterprise, a local newspaper that covers the Upper Cape.
