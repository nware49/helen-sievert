Helen Sievert's work sits where research, reporting, and public service meet. It spans an honors thesis on the political engagement of second-generation immigrants, a newsroom, a law office, and a statehouse internship on Cape Cod.

She graduated cum laude from the Commonwealth Honors College at the University of Massachusetts Amherst with a B.A. in Political Science, a minor in History, and an International Relations Certificate. Her academic writing spans immigration and identity, media and democracy, foreign policy, international security, and political theory.

Today she writes and copy-edits for the Enterprise newspapers on Cape Cod, carrying an academic interest in how institutions work into the daily practice of local journalism.
